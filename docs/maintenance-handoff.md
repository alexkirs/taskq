# Maintenance handoff

The normative lifecycle and recovery rules are in [taskq.md](../taskq.md). This checklist
organizes evidence; it does not authorize maintenance, recovery or resumption. Record each
operational checkpoint on the owning project's board or in its private evidence archive.

## Preserve and drain

1. Record the owner's maintenance scope and notify each existing controller through its
   supported authorized route. Stop new admission and automatic retries at a clean boundary.
   Preserve existing claims, answers, branches, artifacts, handles and guards. Invocation-local
   zero limits do not establish a cross-host pause.
2. Obtain an addressed checkpoint from every owning host: exact task/session, current operation,
   birth-bearing process identity where supported, in-flight effects, board/event/result receipts,
   pending obligations and held guard token. A completed outer turn is not an application drain.
3. Reconcile unknown writers and lost authoritative responses. An absent PID, elapsed timeout,
   inaccessible tab or new tab does not establish that the predecessor stopped or released its
   grant. Keep unresolved slots occupied and preserve questions/history.
4. Use documented exact-token recovery only after identity-qualified drain, outcome reconciliation
   and the required authorization. Never fabricate a legacy handle's birth, spoof identity,
   delete an unknown grant or terminate a shared application server to simulate targeted drain.

## Qualification and resumption

Retain the installed revision/contract and each isolated candidate's exact head, configuration,
checks and artifacts. Keep native platform/tooling failures separate from security policy.
Mocks, read-only reports and process observations qualify only the boundaries they exercised.

Before activation, verify compatibility on every participating host and a bounded canary:
actual receipt, supported action, independent acceptance, next wait where applicable and no
duplicate controller. Preserve rollback and unresolved pending obligations. Publication,
installation and queue resumption require their own existing authorization; a passing repair
test does not release a maintenance hold. Report unknown startup autoresume or unsupported
addressed drain explicitly rather than claiming global quiescence.
